Resume & cover letter

Upload your resume and Job Copilot reads your details from it (name, contact, links, current role, education, skills) so you can review and edit them before saving. The file is stored only in this browser and attached automatically to resume upload fields when you autofill. It is read here, unless you choose Read with Claude (set up under Claude (optional)), which sends it to Anthropic's API.

Saved answers

Answers to recurring custom questions. Ones you type here are matched by similar wording. When you answer a new question on an application and click Submit or Next, Job Copilot remembers it (marked "remembered") and reuses it only for the same question. Answers that mention a specific company are reused only for that company.

Claude (optional)

Add your own Anthropic API key to let Claude read your resume. It handles two-column and designed resumes much better than the built-in reader. When you use it, the resume file is sent to Anthropic's API with your key and billed to your account (with Haiku, a resume typically costs well under one US cent). The key is stored only in this browser and is used only from this settings page. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

Claude bridge

Claude in Chrome can call window.jobCopilot on pages you visit to detect jobs, read postings and autofill. Read-only commands work everywhere. Autofill with your profile and file attachment only work on trusted job sites.
